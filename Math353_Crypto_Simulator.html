Mathematical model

Rising path: Pₙ = P₀(1 + r)ⁿ

Falling path: Pₙ = P₀(1 − r)ⁿ

Where P₀ is the starting price, r is the percentage change written as a decimal, and n is the number of periods.

Investigation questions

  1. What happens to the final price when volatility increases?
  2. What happens when the number of periods increases?
  3. How does the gap between the rising and falling paths change?
  4. Why are these extreme scenarios not predictions of actual market prices?
